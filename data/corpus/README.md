# Corpus files

`dpa.pdf`: Data Protection Act (Cap. 411C), legislation as at 31 December 2022.
Source: Kenya Law and Laws.Africa, https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24/eng@2022-12-31
Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). There is no copyright on
the legislative content. Redistributed here unmodified, with attribution, for non-commercial use.

Kenya Law serves the PDF behind a browser check, so CI cannot download it; this copy was saved from a
browser. `scripts/fetch_corpus.py` uses it and records its SHA-256 in `data/corpus.lock.json`.
